👥 4. Trust Philosophy

Nandika follows the principle:

«Trust should be earned through verification, transparency, accountability and continuous quality improvement—not simply by popularity.»

---

🏛️ 5. Official Source Verification

Clearly identify official sources such as:

- Government Ministries
- Government Departments
- Constitutional Bodies
- Public Sector Organisations
- Government Universities
- Government Schools
- Regulatory Authorities
- Official Notifications
- Official Websites

Display a clear Official Source badge where applicable.

---

🎓 6. Educational Institution Verification

Verify:

- Schools
- Colleges
- Universities
- Skill Development Institutes
- Coaching Organisations (where applicable)
- Research Institutions

Display:

- Recognition Status
- Accrediting Authority
- Official Website
- Last Verification Date

---

💼 7. Business Verification

Support verification for:

- MSMEs
- Companies
- Startups
- Local Businesses
- Service Providers

Display verified business information without endorsing commercial quality.

---

👨‍🏫 8. Mentor & Professional Verification

Applicable to:

- Teachers
- Mentors
- Career Counsellors
- Doctors
- Lawyers
- Chartered Accountants
- Engineers
- Trainers
- Consultants
- Subject Experts

Verification may include identity, qualifications, licences (where relevant) and professional credentials.

---

🤝 9. NGO & Community Organisation Verification

Verify recognised organisations participating on the platform.

👤 10. User Verification Levels

Example levels:

- Basic User
- Verified User
- Student Verification
- Professional Verification
- Mentor Verification
- Organisation Administrator

Verification should unlock appropriate platform capabilities without creating unfair advantages.

---

🤖 11. AnnS AI Trust Framework

AnnS AI should:

- Distinguish facts from opinions.
- Indicate uncertainty when information cannot be confirmed.
- Prefer official and authoritative sources.
- Avoid presenting speculation as fact.
- Clearly identify AI-generated content where appropriate.

---

📄 12. Content Verification System

Content categories:

- Official Documents
- Educational Content
- User Contributions
- Community Discussions
- AI Generated Content
- Partner Content

Each category should follow appropriate review and verification standards.

🚨 14. Misinformation Prevention

Platform measures:

- Prefer official references.
- Flag potentially outdated information.
- Allow users to report inaccuracies.
- Review reported content.
- Remove or correct verified misinformation.

🔄 17. Periodic Verification
