Education Tile — Complete Feature Ecosystem

Purpose

The Nandika Education Tile is a complete lifelong learning and growth ecosystem.

It is not limited to school education, colleges, or certificates.

It supports every user who wants to:

- Learn
- Improve knowledge
- Develop skills
- Change careers
- Gain qualifications
- Explore opportunities
- Build expertise
- Share knowledge
- Contribute to society

The Education Tile connects learning with real-world outcomes.

---

1. Personalised Education Journey System

Every user starts from their current situation.

The system understands:

- Current education level
- Skills
- Experience
- Interests
- Goals
- Location
- Available resources

Entry points:

- School student
- College student
- Dropout learner
- Job seeker
- Working professional
- Entrepreneur
- Researcher
- Senior citizen learner
- Global user

Output:

My Personal Education Roadmap
