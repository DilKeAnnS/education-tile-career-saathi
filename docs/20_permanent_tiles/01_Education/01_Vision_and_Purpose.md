Part 1 — Student User Vision & Purpose

Student Education Ecosystem Vision

The Education Tile of Nandika is designed to support every student throughout their complete learning and growth journey.

The purpose is not to force a student into a particular stream, course, or career. Instead, Nandika provides complete knowledge, all possible options, opportunities, and structured roadmaps so that students can explore, compare, understand, and make their own informed decisions.

Every student has different interests, abilities, dreams, circumstances, and goals. The Education Tile respects individual choices and provides equal importance to every learning pathway.

The student journey begins from their current stage and continues until their desired achievement level, including education, skills, professional growth, specialisation, mastery, and contribution.

---

Student Journey Purpose

The Education Tile helps students understand:

- Where they are currently
- What options are available
- What pathways they can choose
- What education and skills are required
- What opportunities exist after each choice
- How they can progress towards their goals

The platform provides a complete roadmap:

Current Status → Options → Selection → Learning Path → Skills → Opportunities → Mastery → Specialisation → Goal Achievement → Contribution

A student can enter the Education Tile from any stage:

- School education

Part 2 — Parent User Vision & Purpose

Parent Education Ecosystem Vision

The Education Tile of Nandika recognizes parents as an important part of the education ecosystem.

Parents play a major role in a learner's journey through guidance, support, encouragement, decision-making, and resource management. However, parents often face challenges due to limited information about changing education systems, career opportunities, new learning pathways, financial planning, and future skill requirements.

The Education Tile empowers parents with complete, reliable, and structured information so they can make informed decisions and provide better support according to their child's interests, abilities, goals, and individual journey.

Nandika does not make decisions on behalf of parents or students. It provides knowledge, comparisons, opportunities, and personalised support so families can choose the most suitable path together.
