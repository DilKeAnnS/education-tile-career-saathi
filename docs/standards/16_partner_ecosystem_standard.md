Nandika Partner Ecosystem Standard

Version 1.0

---

1. Purpose

Nandika is designed as a complete digital ecosystem that connects people with:

Knowledge

Education

Career opportunities

Skills

Research

Innovation

Community support

Trusted services


To achieve this vision, Nandika requires collaboration with trusted organisations, institutions, experts, and communities.

The purpose of this standard is to define how partnerships are created, verified, managed, and integrated across the entire Nandika ecosystem.


---

2. Vision

> Nandika partnerships should create opportunities for people while protecting user trust, privacy, and safety.



A partner is not just an organisation connected to Nandika.

A partner becomes a responsible participant in the ecosystem.


---

3. Partner Ecosystem Philosophy

Traditional platforms often treat partnerships as business relationships only.

Nandika follows a different approach:

Partner

+

Nandika Platform

+

User Benefit

=

Trusted Ecosystem Growth

The primary objective is:

User growth

Knowledge sharing

Opportunity creation

Social contribution


Not only commercial benefit.

7. Trust-First Partnership Model

Every partnership decision should answer:

1. Is this beneficial for users?


2. Is the partner trustworthy?


3. Is the information accurate?


4. Is user privacy protected?


5. Does it align with Nandika values?

Partners must:

✅ Provide accurate information
✅ Respect user privacy
✅ Avoid misleading claims
✅ Provide genuine opportunities
✅ Follow applicable laws and regulations

❌ Provide false opportunities
