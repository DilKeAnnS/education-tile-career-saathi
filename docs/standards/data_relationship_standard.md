NANDIKA DATA RELATIONSHIP STANDARD

Document Purpose

This document defines how information, users, services, opportunities, experiences, and digital entities connect across the Nandika ecosystem.

Nandika is designed as:

«One Platform for All Kinds of Users — connecting people, knowledge, services, products, opportunities, communities, and experiences through one unified digital ecosystem.»

Nandika is not limited to a single domain.

It supports:

- Learning
- Career growth
- Personal development
- Shopping
- Entertainment and fun
- Services
- Business
- Communities
- Professional networks
- Opportunities
- Future digital ecosystems

This standard ensures that every entity in Nandika can be created once, connected correctly, and reused across all current and future tiles.

---

1. Core Relationship Philosophy

Nandika follows:

«Create once → Verify once → Connect everywhere.»

The strength of Nandika comes from meaningful connections between:

- People
- Knowledge
- Services
- Products
- Organisations
- Opportunities
- Communities
- Experiences

Relationships create intelligence.

3. Core Relationship Principles

3.1 Universal Ecosystem Design

Every entity should be designed so it can participate in multiple ecosystems.

Example:

A user may be:

- Student today
- Professional tomorrow
- Entrepreneur in future
- Contributor throughout life

Nandika should support the complete journey.

---

3.2 One Entity, Multiple Uses

A single entity should support multiple tiles.

Example:

A person:

Can be:

- Learner
- Customer
- Service provider
- Mentor
- Contributor
- Partner

depending on their role and permission.

---

3.3 No Duplicate Data Islands

Tiles should not create separate databases.

Example:

A business should not have separate records in:

- Marketplace
- Jobs
- Services
- Community

One business entity should connect everywhere.
