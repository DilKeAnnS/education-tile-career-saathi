# 1. Introduction

Nandika is a universal digital ecosystem designed to support every type of user throughout their complete life journey.

Nandika is not only an education application, job application, or information platform.

It is a lifelong companion that helps people:

- Learn
- Discover themselves
- Develop skills
- Find opportunities
- Make better decisions
- Grow professionally
- Build businesses
- Become leaders
- Contribute to society


---

# 2. Core Vision

> Nandika — One Platform for All Kinds of Users

The mission of Nandika is:

"To provide free, trusted, personalised and AI-powered knowledge, guidance and services for every person from foundation level to expert and leadership level."
