🛂 8. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Every role receives only the permissions required.

Examples:

- User
- Mentor
- Moderator
- Content Reviewer
- Business Partner
- Organisation Admin
- System Administrator

Apply the Principle of Least Privilege.

🔒 9. Data Protection

Protect:

- Personal Information
- Educational Data
- Career Progress
- Health Preferences
- Business Information
- Financial Records (where applicable)
- User Settings

---

🔐 10. Encryption Strategy

Protect data:

- During transmission
- During storage
- During backup

Use modern, industry-standard encryption practices.

---

📜 11. User Consent Management

Users control:

- AI Personalisation
- Notifications
- Location Access
- Camera
- Microphone
- Contacts
- File Access
- Analytics
- Marketing Preferences

Consent should be clear, informed and revocable.

---

🤖 12. AnnS AI Privacy Framework

AnnS AI should:

- Explain why recommendations are made where appropriate.
- Minimise personal data usage.
- Respect user permissions.
- Avoid unnecessary data collection.
- Clearly distinguish AI-generated content.

---

- Privacy-focused defaults
- Content safeguards
- Guardian features (where applicable)
